Architecture

The weather-to-market pattern.

1. Data source: NOAA / weather.gov alerts
2. Event classifier: heat, storm, freeze, flood, hurricane, wildfire
3. Market map: energy, agriculture, insurance, transport, retail
4. Digest wrapper: ~/.hermes/scripts/stormtrade_daily.py
5. Scheduler: Hermes cron no-agent job
6. Delivery: Telegram
