The checklist

10 questions before automating a workflow.

  • What manual command, website, dashboard, or file do you check repeatedly?
  • What decision or summary do you want from that check?
  • Where does the raw data live: API, CSV, website, bot, email, spreadsheet, or dashboard?
  • How often should the system run: morning, midday, close, hourly, weekly, or threshold-based?
  • Who should receive it: you, a team, paid group, client, or community?
  • Where should it deliver: Telegram, Discord, email, Slack, dashboard, or database?
  • What does a useful alert look like in 10 lines or less?
  • What should never happen: bad data, spam, trade execution, private info leaks?
  • How will you know it worked: received message, updated file, log line, status code?
  • What is the first tiny version that would save time this week?
